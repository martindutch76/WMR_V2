A cause's share of deaths is its death rate divided by the country's all-cause death rate, from WHO Global Health Estimates, 2023 (crude rates, both sexes, all ages). Lottery odds are per single game, from the supplied national lotto odds table. Waiting list and transplant outcome figures come from the registry for the selected country (Australia and New Zealand: ANZOD, ANZDATA, ANZLITR; United States: SRTR/OPTN; United Kingdom: NHSBT; Canada: CIHI; France: Agence de la biomédecine; Austria, Belgium, Croatia, Germany, Hungary, the Netherlands and Slovenia: Eurotransplant). Definitions and time windows differ between registries, so each slide states its own basis; only countries listed have data. The donor risk is the figure you enter, not a WHO figure. Hepatitis B, hepatitis C and HIV/AIDS are never used as death comparators, and causes with a recorded rate of zero are left out.
